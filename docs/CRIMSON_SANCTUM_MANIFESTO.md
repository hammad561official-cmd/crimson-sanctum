# Crimson Sanctum Manifesto v1.0

> A portfolio is read. A sanctum is experienced.

## Core Identity
Crimson Sanctum is an interactive cinematic environment presenting a professional journey through atmosphere, architecture, and careful interaction.

## Principles
- Every effect earns its existence.
- The Sanctum never reveals itself all at once.
- Curiosity is rewarded through subtle discovery.
- Silence has value.
- Technology should disappear behind the experience.
- Achievements remain truthful and unexaggerated.
- The project is designed to grow with its owner.

## Emotional Journey
- Loading: Curiosity
- Intro: Wonder
- Hero: Wonder → Suspense → Excitement → Confidence
- About: Trust
- Skills: Competence
- Hall of Achievement: Respect
- Contact: Invitation

## Mobile Philosophy
Mobile is an adaptive version, not a lesser version. Preserve the same emotional experience while reducing computational cost.

## Final inscription
> Every chapter begins with curiosity.
